♥ Features

Everything the banker used to do on paper

  • 01

    Quick setup

    Add players, set blinds and chip values in seconds.

  • 02

    Live tracking

    Buy-ins, rebuys and cash-outs for every player, with undo.

  • 03

    Chip check

    Instantly see if any chips are missing or extra.

  • 04

    Fair settlement

    A short “A pays B” list, rounded to real notes and coins.

  • 05

    History & stats

    Profit, games played and buy-ins for every player.

  • 06

    Game handover

    Pass a running game to another phone by QR code.

  • 07

    Bomb pot timer

    Get a reminder even when the app is closed.

  • 08

    5 languages

    English, Russian, Spanish, German and French.
